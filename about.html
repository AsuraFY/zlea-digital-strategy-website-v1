About ZLEA Digital Safety

ZLEA Digital Safety is an initiative focused on helping people better understand and manage their personal digital safety.

We provide practical information, free educational resources and individual guidance around accounts, devices, privacy and experiences online.

Our goal is to make digital safety knowledge easier to understand and more useful in everyday life.

Have a question?

If you have a question, need guidance or would like to tell us about a situation you are experiencing, you can send us a message.

You can choose to contact us anonymously or leave your email address if you would like a reply.
