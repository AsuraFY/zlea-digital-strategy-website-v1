GET HELP

Are you going through a difficult situation online?

If something is happening with your accounts, devices, privacy or online life and you are not sure what to do, you can tell us what is happening.

You do not need to know the technical name of the problem. Explain the situation in your own words and we will help you understand what you can do next.

Send us a message

You can contact us anonymously if you prefer. If you would like us to reply directly, you can leave your email address.

Leave this blank if you prefer to remain anonymous.

Send My Message

Prefer to talk with someone?

You can also book a virtual consultation if you would rather discuss your situation directly.

During the consultation, we can take a closer look at what is happening, answer your questions and help you identify practical next steps.

This is not an emergency service. If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services when it is safe to do so.

You may also find what you need in our resources.

Our free guides cover common digital safety questions and situations that you may be able to address yourself.
