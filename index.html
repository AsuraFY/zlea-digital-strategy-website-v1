ABOUT

Digital safety without the intimidation.

ZLEA Digital Safety exists for people who need practical help protecting their digital lives, especially when they do not have the time, confidence, or technical knowledge to do everything alone.

Our starting focus is personal digital safety, with particular attention to women and young people. We aim to make support understandable, respectful, and confidential.
