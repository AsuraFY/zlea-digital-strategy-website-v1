Digital safety is part of everyday life.

We use our phones, accounts and online services for work, relationships, money, communication and many other parts of our lives.

Understanding who has access to your information, how your accounts are protected and what to do when something goes wrong can help you stay more in control of your digital life.

ZLEA Digital Safety provides practical information, resources and guidance to help you understand and manage these situations.
